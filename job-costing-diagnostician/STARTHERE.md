# Start Here

You have a finished job that made less money than you quoted, and you want to know why — and what to do about it.

This folder is the analyst. You don't have to read any of it.

## Three steps

1. **Put this folder inside your project.** Drop the whole `job-costing-diagnostician` folder into a Claude Project (or your ICM, or a Gemini Gem, or a custom GPT). Alongside it, put the **job** you're asking about — a folder with whatever you've got: photos of the estimate, receipts, hours, the final invoice, texts with the customer.

2. **Open a chat with it and upload the job** (or point it at the job folder).

3. **Ask: "What went wrong?"**

That's it. It reads the pile, works out the single biggest reason the money leaked, and hands you back a plain-English report.

## What you get back

A short report in four parts:

- **The diagnosis** — the *one* main reason this job came in light, and how it knows.
- **The full ledger** — everywhere else money leaked, ranked, so nothing's hidden.
- **What to do** — the fix that stops it happening again, and whether this job's money can still be recovered.
- **The corrected numbers** — what the invoice *should* have said, and how much was left on the table.

## What to feed it

Whatever you have. A shoebox is fine — it pulls the numbers out itself and tells you what's missing rather than guessing. The more it has, the sharper it is: the **estimate** (what you bid and your cost breakdown), the **actuals** (real receipts, real hours, what you actually collected), and any **texts, notes, or tags** from the job.

Photos of handwritten notes and receipts work. You don't need a spreadsheet.
